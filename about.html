ABOUT

Product design, grounded in research and shipped with craft.

Miguel Alonso — product designer based in Barcelona, with 10+ years delivering end-to-end UX/UI for software products across gaming, health-tech, sports and social-impact platforms.

I design intuitive digital products and lead creative teams to deliver exceptional, user-centered experiences. With a focus on aesthetics, usability, and innovation, I thrive in fast-paced environments — bringing ideas to life and driving real impact through thoughtful design.

I'm passionate about bridging the gap between business goals and user needs to create work that's not only beautiful, but meaningful and effective.

I strongly believe design should always be a core part of product strategy. A real, transparent partnership between product managers, developers and designers is the right way to succeed.

Throughout my career I've crafted digital products for companies including Telefónica Digital, King (Activision Blizzard), UEFA, Doc Planner, Atrápalo and now Benevity.

Outside of work I'm into technology, music, nature, mountains, trekking and travelling — and I collect graffiti and urban art.
