KingSelf-service supportMobile2017

A consistent, in-game help center for every King game.

Self-service customer support players could reach without ever leaving the game — cutting escalations to King's Customer Service by around 60%.

COMPANYKing
ROLEProduct Designer
TIMEFRAME2017
TOOLSSketch · InVision

Problem to solve

King needed a single, consistent mobile support center for all of its games. Studies show that around 80% of people would rather find a solution themselves than contact a customer service representative — the Help Center is an in-game product that gives players self-service support without leaving the game.

Players can search the Knowledge Base for a solution, or report an issue directly to the relevant customer service team — either the game's own, or King's central Customer Service.

My role

  • Participated in concept, ideation and research — running workshops for insights alongside the player-support team.
  • Conducted interviews with player-support agents and key product stakeholders and managers.
  • Built the first user flows and user stories, plus the interaction and visual design, following King's style guides.
  • Ran extensive user testing on mock-ups using Sketch and InVision.

Achievement
