Reimagining search with conversational design and AI.

An in-progress redesign of Benevity's Goodness search — moving from keyword lookup to a conversational, AI-assisted experience that understands intent, surfaces context, and helps employees act on what matters to them.

COMPANYBenevity
ROLELead Product Designer
TIMEFRAME2025 – present · Phase 2: Design
TEAMProduct Manager · Tech Lead · Designer

Context

Benevity's Goodness platform lets employees search for volunteering opportunities, nonprofits and giving campaigns across a catalog of hundreds of thousands of organizations. The existing search behaved like a plain keyword lookup — broad, generic, and blind to intent.

This project explores what search could be instead: a conversational, AI-assisted experience built on semantic understanding, real-time synthesis and personalization, rather than exact string matching.

My role

I'm leading the design exploration end-to-end — framing the problem, defining design principles for AI-assisted search, and designing the 60% proof-of-concept flows now in review with engineering and product ahead of a broader design-review cycle.

Problem to solve

Discovery surfaced five recurring pain points with the existing search experience:

  • Inconsistent functionality across domains — siloed, rigid, and out of step with modern user expectations.
  • Incomplete or irrelevant results, with little contextual understanding.
  • Poor search accuracy and precision.
  • Limited, hard-to-use filtering options.
  • General usability and intuitiveness issues.

Expected outcome

The goal: someone should be able to find a volunteering or giving opportunity that matches their intent within one or two searches — with results that clearly explain why they're relevant, and let people act immediately (sign up, donate, save, share).

Grounding the design in a scenario

Rather than designing against an abstract feature list, the team built a short narrative around Priya, an employee who regularly supports local animal shelters:

1
Priya is an animal lover. She often supports local shelters and nonprofits.
2
When she searches “donate to an animal shelter,” the results feel overwhelming and generic. She wants an easier way to refine.
3
Priya wants to see, in the search results, proof of impact: stories, outcomes, and exactly how her donation will be used.
4
Benevity AI supports Priya's search by understanding what inspires her to give and what the nonprofit needs most now.

Capabilities

Two capability tracks shape the experience:

NLP

  1. Semantic search — understanding relationships between words, not just keyword matches.
  2. Parses a query's intent: subject, cause and action.
    e.g. Animal Shelters · Animals with special needs · Donation
  3. Conversational interface that keeps refining as the user types.

Real-time data

  1. Data synthesis and summarization.
  2. Prioritization.
  3. Personalization based on user and company signals.
